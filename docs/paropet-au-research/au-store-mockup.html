Why the clip position changes everything

Most harnesses clip on the back. That gives your dog a full-body brace to lean into, which is exactly how a sled dog is set up to pull.

Clip the lead at the chest instead and their own momentum swings them sideways, back towards you. The pull stops working. There's nothing on the neck and nothing on the face.

Fitting, in the order that stops twisting

  1. Loosen all four sliders before the first fit.
  2. Put it on, clip both buckles. verify step-in or over-head
  3. Tighten neck straps first, then chest, then belly. Adjust both sides evenly or it sits crooked and twists.
  4. Two fingers flat under every strap. Tighter rubs; looser twists and can slip.
  5. Lock check: after five minutes of walking, re-check the belly strap. Sliders settle on the first walk. verify
  6. Front clip for training and busy paths. Back clip for the quiet stretch, running and the bungee lead.
  7. Take it off at home. It's walking gear, not a chew toy.

Straight answers

Will it stop my dog pulling on the first walk?

Most dogs pull noticeably less on the first walk because pulling stops getting them anywhere. Some determined dogs take a few walks. That's why you get 60 days, not 60 minutes.

Is this a replacement for training?

No. It's a control tool that removes the leverage while you teach loose-lead walking. We send you The First 10 Walks so the two work together.

Can the straps loosen while walking?

Sliders on any harness can settle on the first few walks. Do the five-minute re-check in the fitting guide. If a strap keeps slipping after that, contact us; that's a fault and we'll replace it.

Does it twist to the side?

Twisting almost always means a size too big or two sides tightened unevenly. Fit by chest, adjust both sides, two fingers under each strap. Deep-chested dogs: watch the greyhound fitting video.

My dog chews everything. Will it survive?

It's tough walking gear, not a chew toy. Take it off at home. Chew damage isn't covered, but we'll tell you honestly what is.

My staffy is all chest. Will it fit?

Pick by chest measurement and tighten the belly strap fully. If it doesn't sit right, swap sizes for free.

Should I use the bungee lead on the front clip?

For teaching loose-lead walking, use the included Classic Lead on the front clip: the redirection is immediate. Use the bungee on the back clip for running and tracks.

Is it OK in the heat?

The webbing is lightweight and breathable. Still, walk early or late in summer and do the ten-second hand test on the footpath.

How do returns work?

Email us within 60 days. We send a prepaid Australia Post label, you drop it at any post office, we refund the full amount once it's scanned in. Size swaps are free.
