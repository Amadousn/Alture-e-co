## 6. Fitting instructions (PDP drawer + post-purchase card)

1. Loosen all four sliders before the first fit.
2. Put it on `[VERIFY B4: step-in or over-head]`, clip both buckles.
3. Tighten the neck straps first, then the chest strap, then the belly strap. **Adjust both sides evenly** or it will sit crooked and twist.
4. Two fingers flat under every strap. Tighter rubs; looser twists and can slip.
5. **Lock check**: after five minutes of walking, re-check the belly strap. Sliders settle on the first walk. If a slider creeps, fold the loose tail back through it `[VERIFY B2]`.
6. Front clip for training and busy paths. Back clip for the quiet stretch, running and the bungee lead.
7. Take it off at home. It's walking gear, not a chew toy.

## 8. FAQ (objections handled honestly)

**Will it stop my dog pulling on the first walk?**
Most dogs pull noticeably less on the first walk because pulling stops getting them anywhere. Some determined dogs take a few walks. That's why you get 60 days, not 60 minutes.

**Is this a replacement for training?**
No. It's a control tool that removes the leverage while you teach loose-lead walking. We send you "The First 10 Walks" so the two work together.

**Can the straps loosen while walking?**
Sliders on any harness can settle on the first few walks. Do the five-minute re-check in the fitting guide `[VERIFY B2]`. If a strap keeps slipping after that, contact us; that's a fault, and we'll replace it.

**Does it twist to the side?**
Twisting almost always means the harness is a size too big or the two sides aren't tightened evenly. Fit by chest, adjust both sides, two fingers under each strap. Deep-chested dogs: watch our greyhound fitting video.

**My dog chews everything. Will it survive?**
It's tough walking gear, not a chew toy. Take it off at home. Chew damage isn't covered, but we'll tell you honestly what is.

**My staffy is all chest. Will it fit?**
Yes, pick by chest measurement and tighten the belly strap fully `[VERIFY D3]`. If it doesn't sit right, swap sizes for free.

**Should I use the bungee lead on the front clip?**
For teaching loose-lead walking, use the included classic lead on the front clip: the redirection is immediate. Use the bungee on the back clip for running and tracks.

**Is it OK in the heat?**
The webbing is lightweight and breathable `[VERIFY C1]`. Still, walk early or late in summer and do the ten-second hand test on the footpath.

**Beach and mud?**
Rinse under the tap, hang in the shade. Machine wash cold when it needs it `[VERIFY C4–C5]`.

**How do returns work?**
