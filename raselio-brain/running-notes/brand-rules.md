## How this brand defines winning

- **Primary campaign objective:** Sales — verified 2026-09-06: every Raselio campaign in Meta ad account 1573950771030463 runs objective `OUTCOME_SALES`, optimised on `offsite_conversion.purchase`.
- **North-star metric:** ROAS / bénéfice net — stated in the team's "Récap mensuel — 3 boutiques" spreadsheet (Google Drive, updated 2026-08-28), whose columns are Commandes, CA, Coût produit, Dépense pub, Marge brute, Bénéfice net, ROAS moyen, Marge %, Statut (Perte / —). Whether hitting a ROAS target is the whole definition of success: not stated — open question.
- **Secondary metrics they still weigh:** CTR and CPC are what the ad naming and batch iteration cadence optimise visibly (verified from the account: 9 batches "V2"…"V9" in 12 days, each a new static concept set); conversion rate on site (Shopify weekly digest tracks it). Not confirmed by the team.
- **When two ads in one ad set diverge on spend:** not stated — open question.
- **Where performance gets read:** in-platform Meta Ads Manager plus Shopify analytics (verified: no third-party attribution tool connected; Northbeam not connected in Parker). Stated posture from the spreadsheet: profit is read at the store level (orders × price − product cost − ad spend).
